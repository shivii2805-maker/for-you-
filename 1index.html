❤️ Happy Birthday, Prafull ❤️

Happy Birthday to the person who became so special to me in a way I never expected. We met randomly at the office, and I never imagined that in such a short time, a random meeting would turn into something so beautiful and meaningful.

From spending countless hours together, our hugs, kisses and cuddles, to laughing over the smallest things, fighting, getting upset, and then finding our way back to each other — every little moment with you has become a memory I will always carry in my heart.

And then came the day you had to leave Gurgaon and move to Mumbai for your career. I still remember how emotional that moment was. We both cried because, suddenly, the person who had become such an important part of everyday life was going away. But somewhere in those tears was also pride — because you were choosing to leave behind your comfort, your family, friends and the life you knew, to build something better for yourself.

I know it wasn't easy for you to leave everything behind, and I want you to know that I truly admire the courage it takes to do that. I hope Mumbai gives you everything you are working so hard for, and I hope you achieve every dream you've ever told me about.

No matter how far the distance becomes, I will always cherish the time we had together in Gurgaon — our laughter, our tears, our fights, our hugs, our little moments and especially the love we shared.

On your birthday, I just want you to remember one thing: you are deeply loved, deeply valued, and your presence has made a beautiful difference in my life.

Happy Birthday, Prafull. ❤️ May this new chapter bring you success, peace, happiness and everything your heart deserves.

And wherever life takes us, a part of my heart will always hold the memories of "us" — especially those beautiful days when it was just you and me, together in Gurgaon. 🥹❤️

Happy Birthday, my love. I'm so proud of you. ❤️🎂
